About us

Dynamark Engineers Private Limited is a private limited company, incorporated on 31 August 2024. It continues the work of Dynamark Engineers, a partnership firm that has been in business since 1982.

The company is based in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India.

  • In business since 1982
  • Incorporated as a private limited company in 2024
